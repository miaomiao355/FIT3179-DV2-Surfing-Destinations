Chart 04 Geographic Land Context — GSHHG / GSHHS

The local file gold_coast_tweed_land.geojson was exported from the GSHHG/GSHHS
intermediate-resolution coastline database supplied with the Basemap Python
package, clipped to the geographic extent 151.00°E–155.00°E,
30.00°S–26.00°S (WGS84 latitude/longitude). Polygon geometry was exported
without tracing, manual redrawing, or movement of measurement stations.

Source: Wessel, P. and Smith, W. H. F. (1996), A global, self-consistent,
hierarchical, high-resolution shoreline database, Journal of Geophysical
Research, 101(B4), 8741–8743. doi:10.1029/96JB00104
GSHHG information and distribution: https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/mgg/shorelines/shorelines.html
