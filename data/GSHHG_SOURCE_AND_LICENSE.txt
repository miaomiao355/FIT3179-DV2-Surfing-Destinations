Chart 04 regional basemap and source record
============================================

Map design
----------
Chart 04 uses full-extent, generalised ABS mainland rings and a locally clipped
ABS state boundary.
The official Queensland and NSW shoreline excerpts are retained for reference,
but are not drawn over the mainland. The previous GSHHG polygon export is not used in the
redesigned map: inspection found three Polygon fragments with rectangular cut
edges, too sparse to describe this coastal region clearly. The old GeoJSON is
retained for provenance only.

Regional extent and coordinates
-------------------------------
The earlier clipped land excerpt covered 152.96–153.98°E and 27.86–28.38°S;
the display mainland now extends well beyond the map viewport. Source queries and
local GeoJSON use geographic positions in [longitude, latitude] order and are
rendered in a Mercator projection centred on the Gold Coast–Tweed region. Data
was queried and clipped on 19 October 2026. The ABS state boundary is a
generalised cartographic representation, not a legal survey boundary.

State areas and border
----------------------
Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), Australian Statistical Geography
Standard (ASGS), Edition 4 (2026), State and Territory Generalised (STE_GEN)
layer. Queensland and New South Wales polygons were locally clipped to the
chart extent; their shared border is derived from the published polygon edges. Polygon rings
were rewound for Vega/D3 spherical geographic rendering, without changing
coordinates, so the regional land area is filled rather than its complement.
For Chart 04 display, chart04_land_simplified.geojson generalises the
NSW mainland exterior ring with a roughly 350 m tolerance, preserving the state
boundary vertices and clipping edges; islands below 0.05 km² are omitted.
The unsimplified clipped ABS geometry remains in chart04_state_areas.geojson.
This display generalisation suppresses narrow coastal details and is not a
navigational shoreline.
The display file, chart04_land_editorial.geojson, uses the full largest
mainland ring of each original ABS generalised state geometry. Its coordinates
match those source rings exactly in reverse order for Vega/D3; no display-ring
clipping or additional simplification is applied. Other components and interior
rings are omitted. The independent ABS state boundary remains visible. The
original ABS service response is retained in .chart04-source-audit/abs-states.geojson.
This small-scale display is not a navigational shoreline.
The service publishes the boundary as a cartographic representation, not for
legal use, under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
https://geo.abs.gov.au/arcgis/rest/services/ASGS2026/STE/MapServer/1
https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/standards/australian-statistical-geography-standard-asgs/edition-4-july-2026-june-2031/access-and-downloads/geospatial-web-services

Previous GSHHG excerpt (not displayed)
--------------------------------------
The unused file gold_coast_tweed_land.geojson was exported from the GSHHG/GSHHS
intermediate-resolution coastline database supplied with Basemap. Its original
WGS84 export extent was 151–155°E and 30–26°S. No shoreline vertices or wave
station coordinates were moved. GSHHG is distributed under the GNU Lesser
General Public License v3; the license text remains in GSHHG_LICENSE_LGPL-3.txt.
Source: Wessel, P. and Smith, W. H. F. (1996), A global, self-consistent,
hierarchical, high-resolution shoreline database, Journal of Geophysical
Research, 101(B4), 8741–8743. doi:10.1029/96JB00104
https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/mgg/shorelines/shorelines.html

Monitoring site reference coordinates remain in data/wave_station_locations.json
and are attributed to the Queensland Government monitoring-site pages linked
in Chart 04. These reference points do not claim a buoy remained at precisely
the same location throughout January.
