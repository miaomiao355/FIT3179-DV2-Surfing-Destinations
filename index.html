Conclusion / Australia’s surfing story

Why Australia Stands Out

Australia’s competition calendar spans established coastal destinations, while January measurements at three Gold Coast–Tweed stations show how wave height, period and direction varied across those monitored sites. AusPlay adds a national participation view: rates differ by age and sex, adult and child participant estimates moved in opposite directions from 2024 to 2025, and about one in seven estimated adult surfers took part in organised activity. The monitored stations and event locations offer focused views of Australia’s surf story, not a complete picture of every coast or destination.
