02 · Wave conditions

What are the waves like around the Gold Coast–Tweed region?

The first section followed Australia’s national event calendar; this one turns to measured conditions at three nearby monitoring sites. Wave buoys record ocean conditions. Significant wave height (Hs) is the average height of the highest third of waves in a recording interval; it describes wave size, not surfing quality.
